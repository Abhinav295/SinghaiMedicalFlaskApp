Who We Are

Singhai Medical Store is a trusted pharmacy located in Samarra, Tikamgarh, Madhya Pradesh. We are committed to providing high-quality medicines and healthcare products to our customers.

Our Journey

Singhai Medical Store was established in 2005 by Surendra Kumar Jain. Starting with a small variety of medicines, the store has grown significantly over the years. Today, we offer a wide range of medicines and healthcare products, catering to the diverse needs of our customers. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has been the cornerstone of our success.
